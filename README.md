## Enter the world of The Black Grimoire

*The Black Grimoire* is an independent dark-fantasy 5.5e-compatible tabletop sourcebook project. Explore forbidden lore, unsettling mysteries, supernatural threats and atmospheric adventures.

The **Thirteenth Bell** is a musical prologue to that world. The complete sourcebook and further updates are presented on our [Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/anilsari/the-black-grimoire-a-dark-fantasy-55e-sourcebook).
