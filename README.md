## Welcome to The Black Grimoire

Enter a world of cursed creatures, dangerous relics and mysteries that refuse to stay buried.

**The Black Grimoire** is an independent, dark fantasy **5E-compatible tabletop sourcebook project**. This repository is the official home for its **free preview material and promotional soundtrack**. Downloading does **not** require an account, purchase or Kickstarter pledge.

### Inside the 20-page preview

- **Three creatures:** The Ashen Witch, The Grave Maw Wolf, The Hollow Lantern Saint.
- **Four forbidden relics:** Magical rewards with dangerous consequences.
- **One mini-adventure:** *The Village Without Shadows*.
- **Game Master reference:** Supporting material for the playable sample.

**More languages:** German and Turkish preview downloads are planned but **not published yet**. Only the English PDF is currently available.
